- **Control, game and information route again** as DEFAULT_FOR_GEOMETRY at the measured solver closure, for the
  geometry their operation names. The verdicts match the 2026-10-14 lineage runs. Nothing is attached: attaching any
  family is the founder's decision.
- **Probabilistic is EXPERIMENTAL and leaves routing.** Beta(1,1) beat clipped MLE 16-11-3 (p = 0.22) and Jeffreys

  recorded it as acceptable in advance; the 2026-10-14 DEFAULT for probabilistic (seeds 1001-1040) did not replicate
  on fresh seeds. It is not re-run.
- **What this is not:** a strongest-competitor admission. The arms are #155's (P-only, pure maximin, always acquire,
  Jeffreys), weaker than the genome library's strongest competitors for these geometries (`pid_simopt`,
  `game_lp_dual`, `bayes_interval`). The genome library labels these four `operation_readmitted`, never
  `rule_admitted`.
- **Latency:** the merged control and game solvers are 50x and 1,600x slower than #155's competitors at the median
  (1.96 ms vs 0.04 ms; 8.19 ms vs 0.005 ms). The verdicts rest on quality, not speed.
