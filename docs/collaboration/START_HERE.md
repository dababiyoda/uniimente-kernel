Unrelated work continues.

## Work in parallel without creating parallel products

- Split by independent outcomes and interfaces; use a separate branch/worktree per work package.
- One implementer per overlapping live concern at a time. Shared mission, authority, spend, appraisal and confinement changes need an agreed split or serialized integration.
- Multiple PRs from one session may be an intentional stack. Record the stack and count sessions separately from PRs.
- Intentional competing experiments are permitted when explicitly declared, bounded and separately evaluated. They must not silently claim the same canonical implementation.
- Keep frozen evaluations identified by source commit, original path and digest. Use unique new run paths; never overwrite another lineage's frozen evidence.
- A useful duplicate is substrate: compare behavior, extract or recombine distinct mechanisms, preserve provenance and negative results, and integrate the justified result into one active owner. Do not delete evidence or keep a second authority plane.
- Do not invent a fresh convergence trunk whenever a base moves. Use the acknowledged integration destination; unresolved ownership blocks only the affected merge.

## Integration and review

The integrator records source PR, exact source head, destination head, disposition
(merged, ported, pending or evidence-only), conflict decisions and verification.
An ancestor test alone cannot prove that a separately ported mechanism is absent.

Capture integration source heads before starting. If a producer moves, list its
new delta as pending; do not claim the candidate includes latest work. Recheck
heads and relevant claims before handoff. Producers keep preparing independent
changes; the integrator batches agreed snapshots instead of chasing moving heads
without a boundary. Do not force-push or alter another session's branch.

Independent review requires a different actual reviewer/session. One model using
five lenses is self-review and must be labelled that way. Material decisions keep
the existing five perspectives, alternatives, exactly two strengthening passes,
dissent, rollback and authority analysis. Keep small fixes proportionate.

## Handoff and resume

Before ending or changing scope, publish: what GREG can now do; exact base/head;
changed files and contracts; commands and actual results; first failures; remaining
risks; frozen evidence locations; integration disposition; next owner/action; claim
release or renewed recheck time. If no receiver acknowledged, say unassigned.

On resume, reread the board, heads and handoff. Do not treat a prior read receipt,
green CI on another commit, or a dated audit as current coordination truth.
Unknown execution outcomes require reconciliation, not blind retries.
