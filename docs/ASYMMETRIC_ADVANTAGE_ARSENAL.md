## 1. Founder thesis (faithful summary)

The advantage is not that the repository contains 55 exotic programs. It is that ordinary, buildable technologies are combined into an institution that behaves unlike an ordinary company:

- it remembers why every decision was made;
- it tests possible futures before acting;
- it grants narrow authority instead of broad access;
- it assembles temporary specialist organizations;
- it learns from negative outcomes;
- it turns successful workflows into reusable institutional capability (Capability Genomes);
- it owns its audiences, proof, software and customer relationships;
- it converts verified competence into recurring revenue;
- it survives the loss of a model, platform, employee or infrastructure provider.

> **Models reason. Agents propose. Humans and policies authorize. The Kernel decides what may become real. Reality determines what was correct.**

The 55 technologies are an arsenal of mechanisms. The Asymmetric Advantage Foundry chooses the minimum set for a particular market failure, assembles one bounded system, tests it, deploys it only through the Consequence Gate, and retains it only when it creates externally verified economic value.

> Competitors build products. UNIIMENTE builds the institutional machinery that repeatedly manufactures, proves, operates and compounds products, capabilities and businesses.

## 3. How the Foundry uses the arsenal

1. **Detect a market failure**: unreliable proof, delayed settlement, unverifiable providers, repeated manual work, rented audiences, fragmented data, a single gatekeeper.
2. **Identify the control surface**: identity, eligibility, proof, routing, workflow, distribution, reputation, settlement, standards, capital.
3. **Generate competing designs**: fastest, cheapest, most defensible, maximum ownership, maximum reversibility, maximum regeneration, do nothing (the eleven-route tournament in `foundry/advantage.py`).
4. **Select the minimum technologies.** A proof-and-settlement business needs identity, capability security, event sourcing, cryptographic receipts, workflows, payments, accounting and outcome reputation — not a game engine, social network or swarm. `FoundryComposer` does this selection and prefers `executable` over `partial` over `target`.
5. **Compile an Advantage Genome**: failure repaired, beneficiary, payer, modules, authority, success evidence, attach/detach, revenue, compounding, kill conditions.
6. **Test before deployment**: static checks, historical replay, adversarial tests, counterfactual simulation, shadow, canary.
7. **Produce external proof** on a real measure: revenue, contribution margin, conversion, time to payment, retention, reliability, CAC, founder time, error rate, dispute rate.
8. **Package** the proven workflow as a Capability Genome.
9. **Compound**: the next venture inherits code, tests, history, customer evidence, reputation, distribution, proof and memory.

### The income loop

DALEOBANKS detects attention, pain and demand → WealthMachine turns the signal into a venture hypothesis → the Kernel governs authority and evidence → the Foundry assembles capabilities → a reversible experiment tests the business → the Consequence Gate controls external action → customers pay → outcomes are verified → successful workflows become Capability Genomes → revenue enters the Treasury → capital strengthens the arsenal → the next business launches faster and cheaper.

## 4. Current reality and red-team corrections

Audited 2026-09-26 against `uniimente-kernel` 04b1b7e, `DALEOBANKS` e2f1ebe and `WealthMachineIntelligence` ec82b80.

1. **Test count.** The README's "172 unit tests green" is the Phase 4 figure (2026-07-20). The full kernel suite at this audit is **574 passing** (582 with this change). Tests are internal evidence for stated versions, never proof of external effect.
2. **Genome registry.** Confirmed: `GenomeRegistry` docstring says instantiation "is a bounded check, not a build". Install, migrate, measure and detach remain Foundry work (#12, #13).
3. **Status labels were unsupported in five places.** Before this audit the arsenal had no evidence binding, and `FoundryComposer` ranks on status. Corrected:
   - #31 Web servers `executable` → `partial`: the kernel serves nothing; organ servers exist, but there is no owned canonical site.
   - #20 Emulators `partial` → `target`: no emulator code exists.
   - #32 Owned social networks `partial` → `target`: distribution is through rented platforms.
   - #40 Mechanism design `partial` → `target`: no incentive mechanism is implemented.
   - #33 Federated protocols `target` → `partial`: DALEOBANKS already publishes over ActivityPub (`services/mastodon_client.py`).
4. **Proof moat (#6) was not real on `main`.** Merkle checkpoints are appended to the same ledger they commit to, so whoever controls the ledger can regenerate a consistent history. **Routed into GREG (2026-09-26):** `greg/anchor.py` timestamps the GREG ledger head at an external RFC 3161 TSA once Alfonso signs `ANCHOR_CONFIGURE`, and `greg anchor verify --witness` checks a presented ledger against a witness bundle and TSA root kept outside GREG, so rewrite, deletion, rollback, substitution or reconstruction within the witnessed prefix is externally detectable. The ledger-internal check alone is self-referential and was shown to accept deletion and rollback. It adopts rfc3161-client rather than building a blockchain. Still open: a run against a public TSA (Sigstore) on a founder body.
5. **Identity (#7) is split.** On `main`, inter-organ signing is shared-key HMAC, so any key holder can impersonate any organ. On the GREG path, every command is Ed25519-signed by the founder key or a delegated, expiring, revocable device key. Residual deficit: move the organ bridge onto per-organ asymmetric keys.
6. **Agent-native commerce (#54) now has real standards to adopt.** A2A is a Linux Foundation project with v1.0 (signed Agent Cards) and v1.0.1 (extensions). x402 has an operating Linux Foundation foundation whose members include major card networks and Stripe. Adopt these rather than inventing an Embassy wire format or payment rail.
7. **What 172/574 tests do not show:** a single accepted external customer outcome, real revenue in the Treasury, or a second venture inheriting a Genome. Those are the only evidence that can confirm the doctrine (§1 falsification).

### Single bottleneck

The arsenal is not the constraint. With 7 executable and 34 partial technologies, the minimum proof-and-settlement composition in §3 step 4 is already mostly covered. The binding constraint is **zero externally verified paid outcomes**. That is the Single Bottleneck Metric: count of paid, accepted, externally verified, reconciled outcomes that entered the Treasury. Build order below serves that metric first.
