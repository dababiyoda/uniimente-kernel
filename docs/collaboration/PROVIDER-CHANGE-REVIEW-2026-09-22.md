3. **Adversarial Reviewer:** provider `authority` rankings, fetched MCP listings, refund notices, and recovery states are all self-reported and can launder evidence, capability, cost, or completion claims. Recommendation: keep provider claims subordinate and require deterministic fixtures plus independent evaluation.

5. **Evidence and Welfare Guardian:** Chrome transcripts, dynamic MCP schemas, search content, and hidden thinking increase privacy, prompt-injection, provenance, and evaluator-dependence risk. Require minimum data scope, raw-artifact preservation, untrusted-content treatment, and human review before consequence-bearing use.
