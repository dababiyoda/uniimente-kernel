# OMNIMORPH and the Asymmetric Advantage Foundry

OMNIMORPH is UNIIMENTE's bounded institutional morphogenesis layer. The Foundry manufactures proposed advantage architectures and reusable competence. OMNIMORPH resolves registered Capability Genomes and composes them into temporary Organ Manifests. Neither component grants authority or executes an external effect.

## Governing separation

- The **Foundry** receives a verified market opportunity, requires all eleven strategy routes, compiles a bounded Advantage Architecture, classifies commercial closure, and seals a reusable Advantage Genome only after paid external proof.
- **OMNIMORPH** resolves the architecture against the canonical Capability Genome Registry, composes an Organ Manifest, runs deterministic simulation checks, and emits an activation proposal.
- The **Kernel** remains the only authority source. Human ratification and a canonical Consequence Gate receipt are required before an organ can be recorded as activated.
- **Reality** determines whether the resulting capability worked. Payment alone, internal activity, model confidence, or simulated success cannot seal a commercial Genome.

## Lifecycle

```text
Opportunity
-> eleven-route tournament
-> Advantage Architecture
-> Capability Genome resolution
-> Organ Manifest
-> simulation
-> human ratification
-> Consequence Gate activation
-> delivery and external observation
-> commercial closure classification
-> immutable Advantage Genome
```

## Opportunity requirements

A Foundry opportunity must name:

- buyer;
- beneficiary;
- pain owner;
- budget owner;
- recurring transaction;
- broken governing state;
- trapped economic value;
- accepted artifact;
- external consequence;
- lawful permission path;
- external evidence references;
- legal operator.

UNIIMENTE is never accepted as the legal operator.

## Strategy tournament

The Foundry refuses selection until these routes appear exactly once:

1. fastest path;
2. lowest capital;
3. maximum ownership;
4. maximum reversibility;
5. maximum regeneration;
6. strongest partnership;
7. acquisition;
8. incumbent compatible;
9. radical simplification;
10. complete removal;
11. do nothing.

The tournament is a proposal-ranking mechanism, not authority. Rejected routes remain available for later revival when evidence changes.

## Commercial closure

The closure classifier uses four states:

- `OPEN`: no outcome record;
- `PARTIALLY_CLOSED`: evidence exists but the commercial or operational chain is incomplete;
- `FALSELY_CLOSED`: payment or internal success is being mistaken for verified customer value;
- `CLOSED`: payment, accepted delivery, externally verified outcome, closed reconciliation, non-negative contribution margin, and external receipt references are all present.

Only `CLOSED` may seal an Advantage Genome.

## Organ composition

OMNIMORPH resolves every required capability against `capabilities.GenomeRegistry`. The requested consequence class and budget must fit inside each Genome's authority envelope. Aggregate organ budget is separately bounded.

Every Organ Manifest carries:

- architecture hash;
- objective;
- legal operator;
- exact capability versions;
- aggregate budget;
- consequence ceiling;
- expiration;
- kill conditions;
- isolated state namespace;
- mandatory human-ratification flag.

Simulation can reject or recommend. It cannot activate.

## Activation boundary

An activation proposal is created only when:

- the manifest passed simulation;
- ratification is bound to the exact manifest hash;
- the ratifier is an accountable human or separately authorized legal principal;
- a signature reference is present.

The proposal status is initially `PROPOSED_NOT_EXECUTED`. OMNIMORPH cannot mark an organ activated until it receives a canonical `sha256:` Consequence Gate receipt. This receipt records that activation passed the existing Kernel authority path. It does not prove later customer value.

## Claims boundary

Passing tests proves deterministic contracts and refusal behavior. It does not prove:

- market demand;
- legal permission in a specific jurisdiction;
- a real payment;
- customer acceptance;
- production reliability;
- profitability;
- external autonomy;
- enterprise security assurance.

Those claims require external evidence and the applicable Kernel gates.
