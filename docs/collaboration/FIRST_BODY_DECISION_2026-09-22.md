## Target effect, simplest route and ownership

The intended effect is a persistent, restartable institution on founder-owned hardware, with one GREG interface, durable goals/obligations, bounded tools and computer use, evidence-backed morning review and future business operation with humans and AI. A successful capability acquisition resumes the original mission; it does not close it. An installed Mac product and authenticated remote phone client are future requirements, not present implementation claims.

Preserve existing Kernel owners: `provenance/ledger.py` and `events/` for history and claims, `policy/` for consequence mediation, `identity/` for workload identity, `egregore/` for goal-facing composition, and organ repositories for specialist functions. The UI owns interaction and local permission requests, never policy. The host owns scheduling, process lifecycle, stop and recovery, never policy. A capability adapter cannot invent a grant. The current #93/#97 sandbox GoalChase is a candidate composition, and current-main #98 shared integrity is the adopted substrate. Draft #70 versus #87 runtime ownership is **unresolved**. Preserve static `DurableWorkflow` as the baseline; OMNIMORPH is optional and must win an outcome comparison.

The smallest testable residual for GoalChase is a sandbox-only supervisor that triggers the existing GoalChase on bounded time slots, survives process replacement through its ledger, honors a local pause, and produces a report with provenance and negative results. It introduces no live Mac install, real founder signature, external notification, model, extra authority plane, or new business workflow. The separately stacked implementation on #97 is contingent on review and current-main integration. For the **first product-facing closure**, the pre-existing read-only local repository audit and morning-review drafts #101/#102 are the stronger starting point; their work must be evaluated and ported before designing a rival audit or review surface.

**Pass-2 strengthening:** keep the supervisor sandbox-only and stacked on #97; independently compare it after current-main #98 integration. Productize later as a signed native Mac shell plus background service, portable Kernel worker process, local IPC and Keychain-backed broker, only after identity and recovery review. Browser/OS/visual control are adapter classes with narrower permission and source trust checks. Every new capability must pass source/provenance, isolation, independent test, attach/detach and authority checks, then resume the original mission. Humans remain people with roles and refusal rights.

Material dissent: the trusted-process GoalChase demo holds a public synthetic key, and current Gate/ledger boundaries do not prove protection against a privileged local attacker. #93/#97 predate current main; a merge probe shows at least a ledger-document conflict. A green sandbox test or hash chain cannot cure these. Unresolved deployment identity, Mac signing, installed service lifecycle and real founder approval require separate review.

**Decision: EXPERIMENT.** The product effect is active founder intent; the proposed runtime composition is a reversible sandbox experiment. Its nearest falsification is a supervised host run over one bounded mission across process replacement with truthful receipts and no duplicate or unauthorized effects. A fully embodied closure requires installation on a Mac, UI closure, interruption/reboot, bounded real capability, founder authentication, Gate mediation and truthful result. Real business, functional capability genesis and scaling closures follow only after that evidence. Merge/activation of the experiment remains a separate founder/maintainer decision.

Rollback: leave drafts unmerged or revert the host addition; retain ledgers, failed tests and dissent. Kill if a second policy plane appears, missing receipt is called success, a worker gains authority from its builder, uncertain work is retried blindly, or founder effort rises without outcome gain. Review trigger: independent review of #93/#97 against merged #98 and a fresh-process bounded host run; Alfonso decides later live graduation.
