# Erratum to the F7 audit (2026-10-15)

The frozen audit (`README.md`, `results.json`, freeze `49b2d58`) is unchanged. Its CONFIRMED verdict on the
200 held-out items stands. This note corrects two statements around it, following the independent review of
PR #179 (findings M12 and L13).

## 1. "About 300 times faster" compared figures that are not comparable (M12)

README line 66 divides PMCF's 0.0043 s by the composition's 1.28 s. The first figure is the algorithm run in
process; the second is the composition through three isolated-worker stages on GREG's path. The frozen v4
README and the `limits` of `results-v4.json` both say these latencies are not comparable.

Like-for-like figures, measured by the review on development items only (not re-run here):
- in process, the composition (compile + CP-SAT) and PMCF take about the same time: per-item median ratio 0.96x;
- on GREG's path, through isolated workers, a one-stage exact genome is about 4-5x faster than the three-stage
  chain (3.7x per item under contention; 5.4x on sequential medians).

The 300x figure was also credited, in the reconciliation record, to `flow_expansion_mcf`. That genome shares
PMCF's formulation but not its algorithm, and has no F7 measurement.

## 2. Solver versions were not recorded (L13)

The audit's freeze hashes three files and records the Python version (3.11.15). It does not record SciPy,
HiGHS or NetworkX versions. The container that ran the audit no longer exists, so its versions cannot be
verified. The versions in the session container that wrote this erratum are listed below. They are the likely
versions, not verified ones:

| package | version |
|---|---|
| scipy (bundles HiGHS) | 1.17.1 |
| networkx | 3.6.1 |
| numpy | 2.4.6 |
| ortools | 9.15.6755 |

A future audit freeze should record `cortex.evaluation.closure.dependency_versions()`, as `run_v4`'s freeze does.

## 3. Spend at equal throughput (M11)

The audit and the v4 scorer measure throughput only. A diagnostic on development items
(`docs/evidence/pr155-f7-spend-diagnostic/2026-10-15/`) finds equal throughput on all 100 items. On 46 of them
the composition spends more than PMCF, with a mean excess of 7.6% of the budget and a maximum of 55.8%. It never
spends less. This is negative evidence for the composition, not a change to the audited verdict.
