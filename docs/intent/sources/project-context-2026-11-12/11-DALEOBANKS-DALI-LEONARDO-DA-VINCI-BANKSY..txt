DALEOBANKS = DALÍ + LEONARDO DA VINCI + BANKSY.
Three artistic forces unified into one identity:
Salvador Dalí —  dalis surrealism. The Visionary: Surrealism, subconscious symbolism, impossible realities, and the ability to make people question what they perceive.
Leonardo da Vinci — davincis mind, The Architect: Sacred geometry, anatomy, invention, scientific rigor, and the engineering of ideas into functioning systems.
Banksy —  banksy’s rebeliousness, The Insurgent: Subversion, anonymity, institutional criticism, and ruthless exposure of exploitation through provocative art.
The synthesis: An artist who sees beyond conventional reality, understands how reality is constructed, and uses that understanding to challenge the systems that exploit people.
For DALEOBANKS, the visual language should be beautiful, intellectually unsettling, esoteric, mechanically precise, and quietly threatening to exploitative power—not to ordinary people.
UNIIMENTE can embody the institution. DALEOBANKS can be its artistic and cultural provocateur.
The underlying philosophy: Expose the machinery of exploitation. Understand it better than its architects. Build something superior.
