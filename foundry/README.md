# Evidence-Governed Media Foundry

## Purpose

Build ratifiable synthetic media companies around evidence-governed Territory Graphs, owned relationships, useful actions, products, correction duties, and Gate-only publishing.

## Buildability contract

- **Existing mechanism:** Territory Graph, hash-bound Ratifier, Consequence Gate, Evidence Ledger, and Whole-Body Closure Controller.
- **Defined interface:** `TerritoryGraph`, `MediaCompanyCharter`, `CompanyFoundry`, and `DistributionLoop`.
- **Bounded authority:** agents draft; humans ratify; every publish crosses the Gate; metrics never create authority.
- **Available dependencies:** canonical Kernel policy, identity, provenance, Loom ratification, and approved platform adapters.
- **Security model:** synthetic disclosure, official APIs only, no purchased metrics, no outrage optimization, no exploitation of vulnerable attention, no fear-based retention hooks, and public correction obligations.
- **Failure modes:** invalid graph, weak/stale evidence, retired node, unratified or edited charter, failed Gate execution, rented attention without owned relationships, or zero behavior change.
- **Acceptance tests:** rooted DAG, exactly one owned exit, disclosure/editorial rules, hash-bound ratification, Gate receipt, public correction, false-closure detection, and kill condition.
- **Recovery path:** suspend publishing, preserve negative evidence, correct or retire affected nodes, reratify changed charters, and resume through the Gate.
- **Resource ceiling:** per-publication budget, evidence floor, graph-size bounds, approved tools, and distribution windows.
- **Operating cost:** content production, verification, correction, platform API, moderation, and owned-infrastructure costs.
- **Legal operator:** a named human or lawful media entity, never UNIIMENTE or the synthetic persona.
- **Handoff:** ratified charter, Territory Graph hash, Gate witness and receipt, distribution evidence, and treasury routing record.
- **Replaceable:** media models, renderers, platforms, and channel adapters may change behind stable charter and evidence contracts.
