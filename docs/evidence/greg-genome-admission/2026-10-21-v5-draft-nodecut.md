## Design knowledge, disclosed

- **The generator was written after the deficit was known.** It produces sparse dependency-like digraphs whose
  relevant S-T subgraph spans the natural natops sizes and goes beyond them. On dev seeds 0-9, 2 of 10 instances
  exceed `flow_maxflow`'s bounds after node splitting (natops held-out: 25 of 1,427, already published). The
  generator's size range (10-160 modules before restriction) was tuned on dev seeds 0-9 only, toward the natops
  development size distribution (median 45 vertices, 116 edges). No held-out seed was generated.
- **Expected correctness against the competitor comes from bounds, not cognition.** Inside `flow_maxflow`'s bounds
  both methods are exact, so every within-bounds item should tie on quality. Any win is expected beyond the bounds,
  where the competitor abstains. The README must report the subregion split next to the verdict. A win there is a
  bounds-coverage result. It is not better reasoning.
- **Latency in process is close, so do not rely on it.** The admission harness's `--dev` run on seeds 0-9
  (`docs/evidence/greg-node-cut-2026-10-21/admission-dev-seeds.json`) measured these medians:
  - wall: candidate 1.0 ms, competitor 2.7 ms;
  - CPU: candidate 0.98 ms, competitor 0.88 ms.

  On the natops development part, the candidate's solve plus verify took 2.0 ms and the competitor's solve 5.3 ms.
  The rule decides on wall-clock medians, which load can move. The same `--dev` run reports "EXPERIMENTAL if
  held-out matched": 2-0 against both the baseline and the competitor (p = 0.25 each), because ten seeds cannot
  reach alpha 0.05.
- **Prediction, not part of the rule:** DEFAULT_FOR_GEOMETRY. The candidate should beat the betweenness baseline,
  which was wrong on 2 of 10 dev seeds and 49 of 545 natops development items. If no held-out instance exceeds the
  competitor's bounds, the verdict rests on the latency clause (candidate median <= 1.25x the competitor's). If the candidate's median is more than 1.25x the
  competitor's on an all-tie run, the verdict is NICHE_CAPABILITY. A REJECTED or EXPERIMENTAL verdict would mean the
  baseline matched it, which would falsify the claim that an exact certificate adds value over greedy on this
  geometry.
- **Through `cortex.reason` both genomes pay about 0.3-0.8 s per call for two isolated processes** (see the overhead
  breakdown in the development README). The admission rule times in-process calls only, as it always has. The
  `reason` cost is the same mechanism for both.

## What the outcome changes, and what it cannot

- A DEFAULT or NICHE verdict makes `cognition.graph_node_cut_exact` routable by the Competency Compiler for
  `{edges, source, target}` requests at version 1.0.0. It does not attach it: attaching is the founder's decision.
- It does not change `flow_maxflow`'s status, bounds or routing. A max-flow request (`{edges with capacities,
  source, sink}`) still routes to `flow_maxflow`. A vertex-cut request routes to this genome, because its inputs
  differ.
- The natops-2026-10-21 verdict and evidence are unchanged. Nothing is re-run or re-scored.
