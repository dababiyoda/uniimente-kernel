Section 16 defines VPL "net of cost/latency" and asks whether routing or composition beats the strongest single intelligence. The frozen rules capped latency (at most 120 s median) but did not net it. They did not separate routing from a fixed recipe, and they compared against practitioner pipelines only where a run happened to include one. This accounting reports those three things.

## What it shows

- **No lift is free in latency.**
  - Every lifting family is slower than its best single intelligence: 1.5x (F1, F4), 1.8x (F2R), 2.0x (F6) and 5.2x (F3).
  - In absolute terms the added time is 0.2-0.8 s per item for the constructed families and 6.9 s per day for F6.
  - No netting rule was pre-registered, so none is applied. `accounting.json` gives the count of lifts within 1x, 1.5x, 2x and 5x of the best constituent's median (0, 1, 4 and 4 families). It is a table, not a verdict.
- **Routing adds nothing within a family.**
  - Where the static recipe (chosen on development items) decides at all (F1, F5, F6), the router chose it on every item and the answers are identical.
  - Where the static recipe cannot run on the family's inputs (F2, F2R, F3, F4), routing's whole measured contribution is choosing a recipe that fits the inputs. That is real, since a single fixed recipe would decide nothing there. It is not evidence of a learned or conditional choice between competing fits.
  - This agrees with P4's demotion of conditional routing.
- **The practitioner baseline is not beaten where it was measured.**
  - F6's composition is 23.5% cheaper than the weekly-mean pipeline in total, but wins only 40 of 90 days.
  - Its edge over the median-forecast pipeline (same genomes, median instead of the q* quantile) is not significant on day count either.
  - F5's composition beat the historical mean (22-8) but not the point-forecast pipeline (19-11, p = 0.10).

## What changes because of it

Future P6 families are pre-registered against `vpl_metrics.validate_preregistration`. It requires, before the held-out partition is generated:
- a static arm that must decide every item;
- a compiled arm through the Competency Compiler;
- at least one practitioner pipeline;
- a latency rule (a multiplier or a value of time);
- for a natural family, the data source, digest and licence.
