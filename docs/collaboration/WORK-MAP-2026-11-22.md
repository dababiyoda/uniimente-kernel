## The check-in

Post this on the work board before any edit, commit or push, and again when you resume. When the pull request exists, give this comment's URL as its receipt's `coordination.task_ref`:

```text
<!-- uniimente:checkin -->
## Check-in: <one-line outcome>
- Session: <actual session or task id> (<client>)
- Prompt: <what I was asked, one or two sentences; no secrets>
- Plan: <how, in 1-3 steps; what I will not do>
- Route: N3 | P5 | <unit id> | process   (greg/path.json)
- Guidance read: uniimente-kernel@<the commit at which you read the guide, e.g. your starting checkout>
- Repository / branch / base → head: <owner/repo> · <branch> · <base sha> → pending
- Files / interfaces: <paths in backticks>; read-only: <...>
- Depends on: <... or nothing>
- Overlap inspected and split: <claims and PRs you opened> (clear: nothing open changes the same lines | split proposed to <session>, ack: <link or waiting> | blocked)
- Acceptance evidence: <what will show it works; results go in the PR>
- Reviewer / integrator · Destination: <session or unassigned> · <branch> (PR: pending)
- Next action / blocker / handoff condition: <...>
- Recheck (UTC): <YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM:SSZ>
```

The map names your session only when all of these hold: the marker is alone on the comment's first line that is not blank; your own `Session:` line, naming one id, comes first after the heading; and the post has none of the words, labels and heading ids below. Post a check-in block only for your own session; to pass a block to another session, leave out the marker line. A post names nobody, even your own, when:

- its text outside fenced code blocks (an indented line is read: inside a list item GitHub shows it as text) says relay, relays, relayed, relaying, relayer, on ... behalf (up to three words between), posted for, or post, posts, repost or reposts this or it as yours, as your/its/their own or as your/its/their check-in (or checkin). Inline code counts, so a genuine check-in that names a file such as `relay.py` names nobody;
- its heading (a `#` line, or a line that is one bold run naming no session id, right after the marker) says hand off, handoff, hand-off, hand over, handover or hand-over (or their plurals), hand, hands, handed or handing then up to three words then over or off, a word that begins with reassign, the word review, reviews, reviewed, reviewing, reviewer or reviewers (each also after re or re-), or for/to the next owner. A review word after answer, answers, answered or answering, with no full stop between, is the post's own reply, as live check-in 7068165767 is. Transfer is not read;
- a line that opens, after optional indentation and an optional `- ` or `* `, with a label, a colon and a space, or with a label in `**` bold, names an id other than your Session id, when the label, in any case, is Posted by, Relayed by, Relay, Relayer, From, On behalf of, Recording session, Implementer, Agent, Agent / claim, Identity, Author, Sender, Poster, Submitted by, Sent by or Written by;
- its text outside fenced code blocks says posted by, submitted by, sent by or written by right before an id other than your Session id;
- or its heading names an id other than your Session id. An ask is the exception: a heading that opens with up to two words and then "ask" (`## Coordination ask`, `## Second ask`) may name ids other than your Session id, as live check-ins 6853648077 and 6858741219 do, except an id right after from, by, · or (, or one the map reads as the heading's signature: then it names nobody.
