# Recursive Collaboration Protocol

This protocol turns collaboration, dissent, founder-intent preservation, and continuous goal pursuit into an auditable institutional process.

## 1. Required review roles

Every material architectural change must receive explicit analysis from five roles. One person may perform multiple roles, but each perspective must be recorded separately.

1. **Builder** - strongest implementation case.
2. **Adversary** - strongest failure, abuse, drift, and counterexample case.
3. **Operator** - deployment, maintenance, observability, recovery, and cost.
4. **Beneficiary Representative** - participant welfare, accessibility, dignity, and externalities.
5. **Constitutional Reviewer** - authority, evidence, legality, reversibility, and shutdown integrity.

Disagreement is preserved. Consensus is not manufactured.

## 2. Mandatory alternatives

Every proposal must compare at least:

- proposed design;
- simplest viable design;
- strongest competing design;
- do-nothing / preserve-current-state option;
- staged or reversible experiment.

Each alternative records benefits, liabilities, evidence, dependencies, migration cost, rollback path, and kill criteria.

## 3. Two upward passes

A proposal cannot be marked ready until it completes exactly two strengthening passes.

### Pass 1 - structural inversion

For every advantage:

- identify how it can become a moat, default pathway, interoperability advantage, proof advantage, cost advantage, or participant-welfare flywheel;
- identify the condition under which the advantage reverses into a liability.

For every disadvantage:

- remove it;
- bound it;
- make it observable;
- make it reversible;
- or convert it into a useful constraint, test, modular boundary, market signal, or governance advantage.

### Pass 2 - adversarial compounding

Re-attack the strengthened design as though Pass 1 were already deployed.

- Find new concentration, complexity, incentive, authority, security, maintenance, adoption, and evidence risks.
- Strengthen the design again.
- A Pass-1 downside may not disappear from the record. It must be resolved, accepted with a named owner and threshold, or converted into a kill condition.

## 4. Merge proof

A material change is mergeable only when the record contains:

- founder-intent references;
- system boundary affected;
- all alternatives;
- five-role debate;
- Pass 1 and Pass 2 maps;
- explicit unresolved dissent;
- evidence and counterevidence;
- tests and verifier strength;
- migration and rollback plan;
- operational owner;
- kill criteria;
- decision: `retain`, `regress`, `kill`, `defer`, or `experiment`.

No document, model output, majority vote, reputation score, reviewer enthusiasm, compute ownership, revenue, technical capability, or successful benchmark authorizes production effects. The Consequence Gate and human constitutional authority remain final.

## 5. Collaboration norms

- Critique claims and mechanisms, not people.
- Steelman before rejecting.
- Preserve contributor lineage and rejected branches.
- Prefer narrow contracts over duplicated implementations.
- Prefer reversible experiments over argument when the decisive uncertainty is testable.
- Escalate irreducible value conflicts to the founder.
- Record negative and zero results.
- Never hide scope, assumptions, unresolved risk, or contradictory evidence.
- Optimize for shared capability and market health, not collaborator dependence.
- Claude, Kimi, ChatGPT, future models, code agents, and human contributors are not territorial owners of competing UNIIMENTE architectures. Their work is cumulative substrate for one institution. Where mechanisms overlap, compare them, preserve provenance, select one canonical owner, and retain useful challengers or historical evidence rather than defending authorship.
- The founder's latest explicit intent outranks model interpretations. Current implementation never silently shrinks an active aspiration.

## 6. Recursive application

This protocol applies to itself. At each major release, audit whether it improves decision quality, contributor comprehension, cycle time, defect escape rate, duplicated work, and founder-intent fidelity. Retain, revise, or regress it based on measured outcomes.

## 7. Infinite Goal Chase — living goal graph

### 7.1 Founder correction

The canonical reconciliation of UNIIMENTE's current intentions is preserved in:

- `docs/INFINITE_GOAL_CHASE_CANONICAL_BACKLOG.md`
- `docs/intent/INTENT-0029-infinite-goal-chase-living-goal-graph.md`
- `docs/collaboration/DELIBERATION-IGC-LIVING-GOAL-GRAPH-2026-08-22.md`

The 198-item list is **not a finite static TODO list**. It is a dated evidence snapshot and input to a persistent goal graph.

Every unfinished active founder intention remains part of the Infinite Goal Chase unless later explicitly:

- implemented;
- superseded;
- prohibited;
- deferred with a trigger;
- conflicted and awaiting founder resolution;
- or lawfully retired by legitimate founder authority.

No contributor may infer:

```text
not implemented
=
not intended
```

Nor may a contributor shrink an aspiration simply because present code, capital, science, hardware, law, or infrastructure cannot yet realize it.

Metaphorical or extreme founder language must be translated into observable mechanisms, tests, interfaces, state variables, evidence thresholds, and staged prerequisites. Unsupported literal scientific or metaphysical claims remain unsupported; the underlying functional aspiration remains preserved where lawful and coherent.

A goal graph is an aid to prioritization and traceability. It is not an authority source.

### 7.3 Infinite Goal Chase loop

The institution should continuously reason through:

```text
unfinished intention
→ identify prerequisites
→ identify the highest-leverage current bottleneck
→ search existing cumulative substrate before inventing
→ research / build / simulate / test the smallest sufficient intervention
→ seek counterevidence
→ verify at the correct evidence tier
→ retain, regress, kill, defer, or package the capability
→ update causal memory and lineage
→ unlock downstream goals
→ recompute the entire graph
→ repeat
```

The preferred unit of progress is not activity, code volume, architecture count, model confidence, or checklist completion. It is a verified reduction in the distance between founder-authorized aspiration and reality.

### 7.4 Single Bottleneck discipline

The presence of an effectively unbounded goal horizon does not authorize unlimited parallel work.

At any given time, the institution should identify:

- the **Single Bottleneck Metric** or smallest set of truly coupled bottlenecks;
- the upstream prerequisite with the strongest legitimate downstream leverage;
- the smallest falsification test;
- the next executable brick.

High dependency centrality is evidence of structural leverage, not proof of business value or authority. Rankings remain advisory and must preserve provenance and counterevidence.

This is a constitutional invariant:

> **UNIIMENTE may recursively expand capability. UNIIMENTE may not recursively expand authority.**

Therefore none of the following automatically creates permission:

- acquiring more compute;
- earning revenue;
- improving model intelligence;
- adding a new organ;
- gaining cryptographic identity;
- obtaining a wallet or credential;
- proving a capability;
- achieving a higher maturity rung;
- forming a Venture Cell;
- operating across more machines or embodiments;
- constructing a descendant.

External publication, messages, contracts, spending, asset movement, account creation, network exposure, real-money settlement, physical actuation, production deployment, regulated activity, and authority changes must remain inside explicit founder-approved standing authority, legal requirements, budgets, and consequence controls.
