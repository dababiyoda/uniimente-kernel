## Small executable scope and evidence boundaries

The implemented path is geometry and evidence/consequence classification → eligibility
and budget → Semantic, Fermi/Estimation, Formal, or Evidence/Causal → adversarial
artifact check → abstain/recommend/handoff → CognitiveReceipt → verified outcome.
The first three are solver organs; Evidence/Causal is a separate empirical route.

Formal artifacts establish encoded properties. They do not establish the truth
or completeness of premises. Preserve formal validity, empirical validity and
legitimate authority as three separate judgments. Formalization incompleteness,
world-unverified premises, contradictory/stale evidence, unavailable methods,
timeouts and unknown outcomes remain explicit. Reverse translation and a second
model prompt cannot establish completeness or independent verification.

Evidence/Causal must distinguish association, prediction and intervention.
Without a defensible estimand, identification assumptions and suitable data it
returns evidence gaps, bounded acquisition/test advice, adjudication or abstention.
It must not manufacture a causal estimate from an explanation or diagram.

Learning is method × geometry × conditions × version. The seed records outcome-linked
bounded attribution and reversible **update proposals**; it does not apply routing
weight changes. These proposals cannot
alter grants, policy, rights constraints, consequence ceilings or evaluators.
Absence of feedback is not success. Disabled families cannot activate themselves.

| Founder objective | Observable acceptance evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Evidence | Reproducible typed artifacts, provenance, fewer unsupported claims |
| Institutional leverage | Actual acceptance, lawful procedural progress, completed remedies |
| Accountability | Traceable authorization/action/outcome; corrections and preserved dissent |
| Deterrence | Reduced exposure-adjusted exploitation, recurrence and retaliation |

| Minimal collateral harm | Separate physical, financial, privacy, rights, reputational, discrimination, third-party, disclosure, systemic and tail-risk bounds |

This seed establishes mechanisms for these outcomes; synthetic tests do not prove
institutional acceptance, deterrence, restitution, physical protection, or a
complete real-world shield. Unknown harm remains unknown rather than zero.

## Exactly two strengthening passes

**Pass 1 — structural inversion.** Typed proofs become a reusable interoperability
and audit advantage; more artifacts can become disclosure risk, so evidence access
and retention stay under existing policy. Heterogeneous routing may reduce cost,
but classification overhead and misroutes can reverse the gain; count all overhead
and retain the static comparator. The genome reuses capability envelopes; a second
registry would split authority, so omit it. Biological language contributes local
bounds, feedback and failure diversity, not animal-named permanent services.

**Pass 2 — adversarial compounding.** Re-attack Pass 1: model agreement can hide
common premises, provenance can make false data look credible, synthetic outcomes
can contaminate real competence, and default routing can entrench an incumbent.
Preserve shared-dependency disclosures, world-unverified states, explicit outcome
verification/attribution, held-out tests and reversible routing updates. Pass-1
cost/disclosure risks remain owned by maintainers: any unauthorized disclosure,
gate bypass, manufactured outcome, hidden paid fallback or authority expansion is
a stop condition. No routing advantage is asserted before matched baseline data.

Unresolved dissent: exhaustive geometry classification, real causal identification,
solver/model independence, legal interpretation, victim-protection efficacy and
open-world developmental repair are not established by seed functionality.
Maintainers own the unresolved implementation and benchmarking risk; Alfonso
retains irreducible policy/value decisions. No reviewer objection is silently
resolved by test counts or architectural vocabulary.
