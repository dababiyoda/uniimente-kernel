- `morphogenesis` -> resourcefully form/reconfigure capability to close a verified CapabilityDeficit;
- `self-healing` -> detect degradation, restore function, verify, reconcile, resume;

- `embodiment` -> governed interfaces into software, devices, AR, robotics or physical operators.

The **capability aspiration is serious** even when the metaphor is not literal.
