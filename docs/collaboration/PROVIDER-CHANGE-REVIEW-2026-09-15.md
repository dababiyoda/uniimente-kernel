## Five-role review

- **Founder-Intent Steward:** retain the capability horizon but keep provider-native sessions, compaction, and permissions subordinate to INTENT-0029 and Kernel authority.
- **Systems Architect:** one adapter seam can consume provider sessions or summaries without duplicating workflows, event history, approvals, or receipts.
- **Adversarial Reviewer:** provider convenience can normalize silent authority transfer; `auto` permissions and durable hosted sessions are the strongest current paths to a second runtime.
- **Operator and Maintainer:** the only direct exposure is the existing DALEOBANKS OpenAI key and non-effectful generation path. Rotation readiness belongs in metadata; no secret or provider account action is justified.
- **Evidence and Welfare Guardian:** signed summaries and provider safety evaluations remain provider claims. Preserve full lineage, protect confidential data from ineligible residency/retention paths, and retain failed/ambiguous runs.

## Strengthening pass 1 — structural inversion

The useful provider features can increase continuity and reduce context cost if treated as replaceable capabilities. Their advantage reverses when a provider-owned durable session, signed summary, permission decision, or cost display becomes canonical. The proposed design therefore binds every provider artifact to the existing workflow, attempt, event, authority, receipt, and cost records; separates price from balance allocation; and forbids automatic credential or routing changes. Merge or adoption remains a founder decision.

Disadvantages preserved: D1 hosted sessions can duplicate the runtime; D2 compaction can erase replay evidence; D3 provider `auto` can execute before human review; D4 key expiry can cause availability pressure; D5 incidents and background work create ambiguous completion; D6 recurring reviews can create governance noise.

Alternatives compared: current baseline/ad hoc review; do nothing; brief-only review; a single narrow provider-adapter contract; wholesale adoption of provider-native agent runtimes; and a reversible fixture-only experiment. The narrow contract plus protocol evidence is retained because it preserves capability access without transferring authority.

## Strengthening pass 2 — adversarial compounding

Re-attacked after pass 1: a mapping layer could still launder provider state into canon, reviewers could overfit to documentation, cost fields could encourage account access, and a standing compatibility harness could become a parallel scheduler. The strengthened design makes provider state append-only evidence, requires independent or deterministic verification, records account facts only when separately authorized, creates no standing runtime, and keeps experiments fixture-only until a separate founder decision.

Dispositions: D1 resolved by the provider-as-leased-capability rule; D2 resolved by raw-block plus source-range lineage; D3 prohibited for consequence-bearing tools; D4 deferred to the credential owner with expiry metadata only; D5 resolved by canonical receipt reconciliation and recovery canaries; D6 accepted with the no-material-change/no-PR rule and review after any low-signal PR.

Residual risks: live configurations can differ from repositories; provider documentation can change in place; no provider API or billing account was exercised; and one model performed the five perspectives. These remain visible and require human/independent review before promotion.

## Dissent, migration, rollback, and kill criteria

Material dissent: even docs-only weekly edits can normalize model-authored policy and consume founder attention. Resolve only through founder review and evidence that the packet prevents a missed deadline or unsafe retry without producing low-signal PRs.

Migration: none. Any future provider feature must enter through a narrow adapter and canonical contract fixture; a separate reviewed change must map history, receipts, authority, data class, and cost before a sandbox experiment.

Rollback: leave the draft unmerged or revert its docs-only commits. Preserve the review and dissent as historical evidence even if the protocol change is rejected.

Kill this proposal or any follow-on experiment on any default-branch write, merge, production/credential/billing-account change, provider-owned canonical state, second scheduler/runtime, lost event lineage, missing receipt, duplicate effect, false provider permission allow, ineligible residency/retention, same-provider sole evaluation, or one immaterial recurring PR.
