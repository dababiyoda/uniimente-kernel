## Reconciliation rules

1. **GREG family ≠ enabled cortex organ ≠ reserved profile.** A bounded GREG solver can be implemented and tested while the corresponding cortex family remains reserved/disabled. The matrix says both; it never infers enablement from a shared family name.
2. **Installation ≠ intelligence.** A row is working only to the extent that callable contracts, routing, verification, tests and integration are cited. A library or destination alone yields no implemented status.
3. **Evaluation ≠ reality.** Frozen suites, mutation checks, simulations and fresh-clone runs remain at their actual evidence tiers. No simulated baselines are used. `always_llm`/`llm_committee` remain `NOT_RUN` without the real configured model.
4. **Duplicates receive one canonical home, materially different concepts remain separate.** Constraint/CP-SAT and optimization/OR share solver work but retain distinct rows and lineage (A09 versus B08). VOI and cognitive metabolism are linked but distinct (A12/B17 versus A38). Bounded composition and N3 temporary-tissue promotion are not the same claim. Neural-ensemble/global-workspace terminology is explicitly reconciled on the shared-workspace row. Future substrates are places cognition runs, not new intelligences.
5. **Superseded lineage is preserved.** CP-SAT 0.2.0 supersedes the disabled 0.1.0 profile without deleting it. Backcast position does not determine causal buildability. N1 remains a local evaluation-only gate, not a global stopping condition.
6. **Authority does not grow with capability.** Building, qualification, registration and routing eligibility create no execution/consequence authority. Financial, legal, normative, irreversible, institutional and human-led protection actions remain handoffs.
7. **Negative evidence stays visible.** Abstentions, reserved profiles, skipped real-checkout tests, unavailable models, zero outcomes, failed verifier runs and unproven N3/N1 claims are recorded rather than averaged into confidence.
