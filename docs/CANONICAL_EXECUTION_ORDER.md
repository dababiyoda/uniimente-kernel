## Blocker discipline

When blocked, complete all remaining unblocked work first, then state the exact
credential, authorization, legal decision, payment, customer action or
physical-world event required. Known blocker classes that no build session can
supply:

- constitutional ratification by the founder;
- private credentials and API keys;
- live platform authorization;
- legal decisions;
- payment accounts;
- customer consent;
- real purchases;
- external buyer responses;
- physical-world deployment.

## Evidence standard

Do not claim that anything is complete unless every completion condition has
objective evidence: an executable test, a recorded verifier run, a ledgered proof
artifact, or an externally observable consequence. A claim without evidence is
recorded as a claim, never as truth.
