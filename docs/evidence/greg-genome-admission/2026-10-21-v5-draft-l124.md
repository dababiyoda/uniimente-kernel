## Design knowledge, disclosed

- Each genome was built and inspected on dev seeds 0-9, and checked on review seeds 9400-9439 in the unit tests. No
  parameter was changed after the dev results were seen (`docs/evidence/greg-genome-l124-absent-2026-10-21/README.md`).
- The token bucket's ground truth (offline optimum) and the auction's (transportation LP) both come from SciPy HiGHS;
  neither arm uses HiGHS.
- The diffusion competitor and the Layer 3 `community_louvain` genome both use NetworkX Louvain.

## What the outcome changes, and what it cannot

- A DEFAULT or NICHE verdict makes a genome routable at version 1.0.0. It does not attach it; attaching is the
  founder's decision.
- SUPERSEDED, EXPERIMENTAL and REJECTED verdicts exclude a genome from routing, and are reported as prominently as
  positive ones.
- Every benchmark here is generated (three simulated worlds, one mathematical instance). A positive verdict says
  nothing yet about GREG's real queue, jobs or claim graphs.
