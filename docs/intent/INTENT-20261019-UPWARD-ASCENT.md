## 3. Convergence must produce a capability dividend

Convergence means more than selecting a winning branch, keeping both implementations, moving files, adding adapters or removing repeated code. Those can be necessary steps. The product objective is a measurable **capability dividend**: after the relevant mechanisms are transformed and combined, actual GREG can perform a useful function, maintain a stronger verified state, recover from a broader failure class, complete a new transaction, or achieve a better outcome than the unchanged sources could.

Treat the entirety as potential substrate: working code, duplicate implementations, unused functions, experiments, failed hypotheses, prose, diagrams, tests, negative results, branch history, model disagreements and external mechanism sources. A source's original failure does not establish that every primitive inside it is useless.

**PRESERVE -> EXTRACT -> MUTATE -> RECOMBINE -> BUILD -> INTEGRATE -> VERIFY -> COMPOUND.**

The obligation applies to useful information and mechanisms, not to retaining every byte in an active runtime. Exact copies share a lineage record; their underlying mechanism is evaluated once as a source group. Secrets, malicious payloads, inaccessible data and material without reuse rights are not a license to execute, disclose or redistribute them. Preserve lawful provenance and abstract lessons where appropriate.

## 4. Mandatory Mechanism Recombination Foundry treatment

For each material duplication or not-yet-elevated source group, use Mechanism Recombination Foundry before declaring its product disposition complete:

1. Inspect both/all source implementations and their consumers. Extract primitives, maintained state, transitions, assumptions, information flows, incentives, authority boundaries, resource costs, failure behavior, recovery and proof. A filename or similar API does not establish semantic equivalence.
2. Identify complementary strengths and the higher-order founder effect they can serve. Search adjacent source groups and existing capabilities before inventing a new framework.
3. Explore at least three materially distinct mutations per core mechanism. Include a meaningful change in incentive, authority boundary or consequence behavior, without creating authority. For a recombination claim, at least two mechanisms must be materially transformed and reciprocally adapted.
4. Define the resulting interaction precisely. What does the combination accomplish that neither unchanged source did alone? An unchanged integration must be labeled integration, not invention.
5. Build the smallest complete implementation of that interaction inside the existing canonical architecture. A new name, manifest, wrapper, capability count or declaration of novelty is not the result.
6. Preserve source lineage and negatives. Compare the implementation with the best relevant original implementation, simple composition, and the strongest practical simpler alternative. Measure benefits and costs under the same conditions.
7. Show the new function on a real GREG execution path appropriate to the claim. Re-observe the outcome, preserve the evidence and resume the parent mission.

'Overpowered' means a defensible functional advantage: broader reliable capability, stronger evidence, faster useful execution, lower total cost or founder burden, better recovery, or lawful economic/institutional leverage. Name the baseline, material improvement threshold and non-regression floors before testing. Do not invent multipliers or claim universal superiority across every dimension.

## 6. Conservation and subtraction tests

Conserve every active intended effect and useful demonstrated behavior. Integration must not silently erase specialist methods, interfaces, recovery paths, negative evidence, historical alternatives or future capability horizons. Where two methods differ, record both their strengths and their conditions of failure.

For every claimed capability dividend, remove or disable the alleged new interaction and test what is lost. A benchmark or negative-control capability counts as an implemented contribution only when an actual consumer invokes it and its absence permits a documented failure or removes a useful decision signal. It does not count as completion of the original failed goal.

No gain, regression and inconclusive are valid experimental results. They are not completion. When a particular mutation fails, preserve the failure, revise the hypothesis or mechanism and continue the highest-value unblocked build. Temporary retention must name the unresolved effect, tested alternatives, owner, exact blocker or next test, and revisit trigger. 'Archived', 'historical', 'research-only' and 'future work' are not blanket exemptions from the elevation obligation.

Do not hold urgent repairs hostage to speculative novelty. Land an authorized prerequisite as a prerequisite, accurately report the behavior it repairs, and retain the parent capability-dividend obligation. Never claim maintenance alone satisfies an invention requirement.
