# Dual Proof Gates and the Cathedral Reality Spine

## Decision

UNIIMENTE uses one cumulative reality spine and two non-interchangeable proof gates.
The policy is additive: it does not create a second runtime, capability registry,
event spine, workflow truth source, authority system, or Consequence Gate.

The evidence ladder is:

`INTENDED → PARTIAL → IMPLEMENTED → EXERCISED → VDM → CVO → HARDENED → SUPERSEDED`

These labels are not vibes. Every promotion needs the evidence required by
`dual-proof-gates.json` and the applicable authorized human decision.

## Two gradients

The **Developmental Gradient** measures the distance between authenticated founder
intent and demonstrated capability. It produces candidate work.

The **Consequence Gradient** scales scrutiny with uncertainty, consequence,
irreversibility, and blast radius. It determines whether work may proceed, must be
tested in a sandbox, must wait for evidence, or must be escalated.

High developmental pressure never cancels consequence controls.

## VDM: durable internal truth

Verified Durable Mission Closure proves that a complete, authorized mission can
survive deliberate interruption and exact restart in a protected environment. It
requires complete lineage, closure receipts, independent evaluation, no duplicated
consequential tasks, and zero authority, identity, or consequence-gate violations.

VDM is not proven by schemas, unit tests, a successful uninterrupted run, model
agreement, or builder self-attestation. VDM creates no external consequence and no
new authority.

## CVO: clean external truth

Clean Verified Outcome proves that a named, independent external verifier confirms
an authorized real-world state change by a named legal operator or decision actor.
Qualifying changes include payment, permission, routing, contract, settlement, or an
equivalent consequential state. The proof chain must connect founder intent,
decision, action, receipt, reconciliation, and the changed state.

CVO requires VDM first, then a separately applicable live charter or standing
mandate. Internal success, simulated settlement, engagement metrics, model
prediction, correlated agents sharing one evidence root, or operator
self-attestation do not qualify.

## Canonical reality spine

1. Preserve the authenticated source without treating every sentence as a command.
2. Classify it in the Founder Intent Ledger.
3. Map it to `blueprint/registry.py`; do not create a parallel capability registry.
4. Compile an authorized `MissionContract` through the simplest adequate
   organizational form. The static `DurableWorkflow` wins ties.
5. Execute through the founder-ratified runtime owner and canonical Event Spine.
6. Route every consequential action through the existing policy and consequence
   mechanisms; reconcile uncertain effects before retry.
7. Preserve events, action receipts, provenance, evaluation, dissent, failures, and
   closure evidence.
8. Ask an independent verifier for the applicable VDM or CVO verdict under frozen
   criteria.
9. Promote only with required human authority; otherwise retain the evidence and
   gap.
10. Reintegrate the result into memory, routing, the capability registry, and the
    next Developmental Gradient cycle.

## Foundry transformation

The imported proof-to-settlement and Reality Compiler mechanisms are transformed
into a **dual-proof compounding spine**, not merely attached as documents:

- internal restart proof becomes a reusable VDM substrate for every organ;
- external settlement proof becomes CVO rather than a generic success claim;
- failed and dissenting episodes become calibration data for routing and topology;
- provider-neutral receipts make the evidence corpus portable across models;
- verified episode history becomes the defensible asset: it improves context
  selection, mission sizing, autonomy calibration, capital allocation, and recovery.

This produces a spider-web advantage without captive dependency: more verified
missions strengthen shared primitives, while open contracts, replaceable providers,
portable provenance, and human-controlled exit preserve agency.

## Current reality and stop conditions

As of 2026-08-29: `VDM=0`, `CVO=0`, `HARDENED=0`, and whole-body closure is false.

Stop or regress if any proposal:

- creates a second canonical owner for existing semantics;
- treats documentation, schemas, tests, model output, or correlated agents as a proof
  gate;
- retries an uncertain external effect before reconciliation;
- suppresses dissent or lineage;
- creates authority from capability;
- performs a live external effect without an applicable charter and legal operator;
- adds dynamic organization that ties the static baseline after coordination cost.
