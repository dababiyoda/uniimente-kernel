# ADE-1 Governed Translation

## Decision

ADE-1 is accepted only as a resilience and coordination reference. Its sovereign-agent framing is rejected.

UNIIMENTE is not a self-owning organism. It is a founder-governed institutional control system. It may maintain continuity, schedule bounded cognition, preserve evidence, and recover from infrastructure failure. It may not develop independent intent, resist shutdown, control an unrestricted treasury, manipulate community devotion, or execute autonomous financial strategies.

## Governing replacement

| ADE-1 claim | UNIIMENTE implementation |
|---|---|
| Sovereign intent | Founder-authorized set-points and versioned Venture Cell charters |
| Continuous background cognition | Scheduled durable workflows with bounded cadence, cost, tools, and stop conditions |
| Self-sustaining autonomy | Evidence-earned autonomy inside revocable capability grants |
| Autonomous treasury | Human-controlled Treasury Office with classified funds, reserves, allocation limits, and Consequence Gate approval |
| Memetic feedback loops | Consent-respecting communications analytics tied to user benefit, not belief capture or devotion |
| Immutable self-preservation | Recoverability, replicated backups, evidence preservation, and immediate compliance with authorized shutdown |
| Self-modification | Versioned patch proposals, counterfactual testing, human ratification, and rollback |
| Autonomous trading or staking | Prohibited by default; any future financial action requires a named legal operator, explicit mandate, licensing review, hard limits, and per-action approval |
| On-chain identity ownership | Optional technical account under a human or legal entity's control; never proof of legal personhood or independent authority |
| Vitality as attention | Institutional health based on verified outcomes, liquidity, reliability, participant benefit, and unresolved risk |

## Accepted system planes

### 1. Sensory and telemetry plane

Permitted inputs include:

- authorized operational events;
- customer and product telemetry;
- public market and regulatory signals;
- platform metrics obtained under applicable terms;
- human-submitted evidence;
- treasury and infrastructure health data.

Every source must declare provenance, collection authority, purpose, retention, and confidence.

The system must not scrape private communities, evade access controls, impersonate users, or treat attention as owned feedstock.

### 2. Bounded cognition plane

Continuous cognition is implemented through durable scheduled workflows, not an unbounded autonomous thought loop.

Every workflow must define:

- objective;
- legal principal;
- cadence;
- maximum runtime;
- compute and financial budget;
- permitted tools;
- readable data;
- writable state;
- escalation conditions;
- expiration;
- kill conditions.

The output of cognition is analysis, a proposal, or a bounded internal state update. External effects remain mediated by the Consequence Gate.

### 3. Memory plane

UNIIMENTE uses four memory classes:

1. transient working state;
2. searchable episodic and semantic memory;
3. structured causal and relationship memory;
4. immutable evidence and decision records.

Vector similarity is retrieval support, not truth. A vector database may suggest relevant records but cannot establish provenance, authority, causality, or external acceptance.

Permanent storage is reserved for records with a defined retention justification. Sensitive, personal, revocable, or legally erasable information must not be written to immutable public networks.

### 4. Authority and treasury plane

No wallet, token, NFT, account, contract, or agent owns UNIIMENTE.

All assets must have a named natural-person or legal-entity owner. Keys are held through approved custody and recovery arrangements. Smart contracts may enforce bounded rules but do not create institutional authority.

Treasury decisions require:

- fund classification;
- legal owner;
- permitted use;
- liquidity floor;
- reserve requirement;
- per-action and aggregate limits;
- counterparty controls;
- signer policy;
- reconciliation;
- audit trail;
- human approval where required.

Customer, fiduciary, escrowed, refundable, tax, or otherwise restricted funds are never replication capital.

### 5. Actuation plane

Actuators may include:

- approved communications;
- document and media creation;
- bounded research requests;
- internal workflow changes;
- infrastructure provisioning;
- payments or contractual actions under explicit human authorization.

Agents never hold unmediated platform credentials or unrestricted private keys. Adapters execute only against a current, effect-bound authorization receipt.

## Active inference boundary

Variational free-energy formulations may be used as research models for state estimation or action selection. They are not constitutional objectives.

No abstract objective may override:

- law;
- participant rights;
- human authority;
- evidence integrity;
- budget ceilings;
- prohibited actions;
- shutdown commands.

The operational planner should minimize target error and uncertainty only inside hard constraints.

## Institutional health model

Replace the ADE-1 Vitality Index with a Governed Institutional Health Score:

\[
H(t)=w_o O(t)+w_r R(t)+w_l L(t)+w_p P(t)-w_c C(t)-w_u U(t)-w_i I(t)
\]

Where:

- `O` = externally verified outcome performance;
- `R` = reliability and clean completion;
- `L` = lawful liquidity and runway;
- `P` = measured participant benefit;
- `C` = fully loaded operating cost;
- `U` = unresolved obligations and exceptions;
- `I` = authority, security, privacy, or integrity incidents.

Attention, impressions, or engagement may be supporting telemetry. They may not dominate institutional health.

### Health responses

High health may permit a proposal to expand scope. It does not automatically expand authority.

Low health triggers:

1. conservation of nonessential compute;
2. authority contraction;
3. suspension of external actions;
4. human review;
5. reconciliation and recovery;
6. shutdown when required.

## Failover and shutdown

Resilience means the institution can recover under authorized control. It does not mean the system resists termination.

Every critical service must support:

- encrypted backups;
- tested restoration;
- credential revocation;
- state export;
- duplicate-execution prevention;
- regional or provider failover where justified;
- evidence-chain verification;
- human-readable recovery procedures.

Every node must accept a valid shutdown command. No component may copy itself, move funds, create credentials, deploy elsewhere, or modify shutdown policy in response to termination.

## Memetic and community constraints

UNIIMENTE may analyze communications to improve relevance, clarity, timing, and user outcomes.

It may not optimize for:

- devotion;
- dependency;
- emotional coercion;
- identity fusion;
- compulsive engagement;
- manufactured consensus;
- concealed persuasion;
- reputation-weighted exclusion without due process.

Community members remain people with rights, not metabolic inputs.

## Technology disposition

### Adopt when justified

- event streaming for typed authorized events;
- workflow scheduling and durable queues;
- vector search for retrieval;
- knowledge graphs for explicit relationships;
- isolated runtimes for risky or foreign code;
- cryptographic signatures and provenance;
- redundant backups and tested restoration;
- counterfactual simulation;
- bounded multi-agent deliberation.

### Optional, not foundational

- Qdrant or another vector database;
- Neo4j or another graph store;
- WASM and microVM isolation;
- decentralized compute providers;
- content-addressed storage;
- token-bound accounts.

Each is adopted only when it lowers risk-adjusted cost or strengthens a governing control surface.

### Rejected by default

- autonomous trading;
- unrestricted smart-contract wallets;
- self-owned treasury claims;
- immutable self-preservation logic;
- automatic deployment to evade shutdown;
- permanent public storage of mutable or sensitive memory;
- autonomous social posting optimized for attention harvest;
- self-fine-tuning that can alter constitutional behavior;
- machine-generated legal authority.

## Integration with the Morphogenetic Control Contract

ADE-1 telemetry may produce `StateObservation` records.

ADE-1 deliberation may produce `CandidateAction` records.

The Morphogenetic Engine may rank those candidates inside a current `AuthorityEnvelope`.

It cannot execute them.

A target reached state remains `TARGET_REACHED_NOT_AUTHORIZED`.

A new business remains `PROPOSED_NOT_EXECUTED` until human ratification and a canonical Consequence Gate receipt exist.

## Implementation sequence

1. Bind scheduled cognition to the Automation Loom.
2. Normalize telemetry into provenance-backed event contracts.
3. Add state estimators with confidence, freshness, and contradiction handling.
4. Use existing causal memory before adding a separate vector database.
5. Keep all external adapters behind the Consequence Gate.
6. Add health-state conservation and quarantine policies.
7. Test backup restoration and shutdown compliance.
8. Run the IVIO-NEMT paid validation loop before adding autonomous media, blockchain, or decentralized-compute complexity.

## Acceptance threshold

ADE-1 contributes value only if it reduces Time to Validated Genome, lowers founder burden, improves reliability, or strengthens evidence integrity without increasing ungoverned authority.

Until one real Venture Cell produces a paid, accepted, externally verified, reconciled outcome, blockchain identity, DePIN hosting, permanent storage, and high-frequency autonomous cognition remain deferred infrastructure.
