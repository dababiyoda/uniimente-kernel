## Canonical morphogenesis interpretation

`morphogenesis` means **functional capability formation or reconfiguration in response to a verified discrepancy or CapabilityDeficit**.

It does not mean that digital cells, tissues, developmental fields, local rules, decentralized topology, or dynamically generated organizations must be built. Those mechanisms remain available when they outperform simpler alternatives.

`developmental/` is a mechanism-research laboratory. `morphogenesis/` is one target-state control lineage. OMNIMORPH is an optional organization-design/composition mechanism. Functional-replacement experiments are useful substrate for capability genesis. None individually defines UNIIMENTE.

## Blocker discipline

When blocked, complete all remaining unblocked work first, then state the exact credential, authorization, legal decision, payment, customer action, missing technology or physical-world event required. Do not silently replace a blocked aspiration with a smaller goal.

Known blocker classes that no build session can supply on its own include:

- constitutional ratification by the founder;
- private credentials and API keys;
- live platform authorization;
- legal decisions;
- payment accounts;
- customer consent;
- real purchases;
- external buyer responses;
- physical-world deployment;
- capabilities not yet available at required reliability.

## Evidence standard

Do not claim that anything is complete unless every completion condition has objective evidence: an executable test, a recorded verifier run, a ledgered proof artifact, or an externally observable consequence appropriate to the claim.

A claim without evidence is recorded as a claim, never as truth. A simulation that resembles a metaphor does not prove the underlying effect.

Every material capability-formation claim should preserve:

- founder-intent lineage;
- target effect;
- mechanism provenance;
- alternatives searched;
- residual deficit;
- authority envelope;
- tests and hostile controls;
- known failures;
- independent verification or explicit absence;
- attach/detach/rollback;
- outcome evidence.
