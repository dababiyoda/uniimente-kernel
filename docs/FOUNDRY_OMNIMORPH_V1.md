# Advantage Foundry and OMNIMORPH v1

Status: integration candidate  
Source lineage: PRs #27 and #29  
Canonical target: `integration/uniimente-egregore-v1`

## Governing conclusion

UNIIMENTE now separates five different artifacts that previous branches partially conflated:

1. **OpportunitySpec** — a governing market transaction accepted from Phase Zero only after canonical contract validation and explicit human-approved supplemental facts.
2. **Advantage Architecture** — the selected design produced after all eleven strategic routes are generated and preserved.
3. **Composition Plan** — a reversible, non-executing dependency plan selecting the minimum feasible subset of the 55-technology arsenal.
4. **Organ Manifest** — OMNIMORPH's temporary bounded body plan with Capability Genome bindings, budget, consequence ceiling, expiration, kill conditions, and isolated state namespace.
5. **Sealed Advantage Genome** — an immutable reusable institutional asset created only after a clean paid, accepted, externally verified, economically reconciled outcome.

No earlier artifact may masquerade as a later one.

## Authority invariant

The following statements are simultaneously true:

- the Foundry may rank plans but may not execute them;
- the Tribunal may reject or require reconfiguration but may not authorize;
- OMNIMORPH may compose and simulate but may not activate itself;
- human ratification is necessary but not sufficient for activation;
- a Gate receipt is necessary but not sufficient for market validation;
- payment is necessary but not sufficient for commercial closure;
- only an accountable human-approved RETAIN decision after clean external closure may seal a Genome.

## Non-zero-sum collaboration policy

- source PRs and commit lineage are preserved;
- useful mechanisms are credited and normalized rather than overwritten;
- duplicate implementations remain historical or fallback evidence;
- negative routes and countercases remain available for future conditions;
- shared commodity mechanisms remain replaceable;
- participant capability, informed consent, proof quality, and fair settlement are design requirements;
- no attention manipulation, authority laundering, evidence fabrication, or data hostage-taking.
