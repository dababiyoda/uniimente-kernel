# UNIIMENTE Asymmetric Advantage Foundry

## 1. Command Intent
UNIIMENTE operates as a human-sovereign artificial institution. Its sole function is to continuously manufacture durable, lawful, evidence-proven unfair advantages. It discovers, constructs, validates, protects, and compounds advantages while strictly maintaining the founder's sovereign authority and strategic freedom.

## 2. Governing Objective
Convert perception, knowledge, labor, and capital into owned capabilities, recurring cash flow, and trusted institutions. Growth is strictly defined by improvements in the real world (verified customer value, margin, resilience, operational reliability), not internal activity metrics.

## 3. The Advantage Standard
A lawful asymmetric advantage must be: externally valuable, owned, compounding, difficult to copy/bypass, portable, economically positive, resilient, lawful, and verifiable.

## 4. Foundry Architecture
The Foundry operates as an isolated organ containing:
- Opportunity Intake
- Hypothesis Generator
- Advantage Tribunal
- Advantage Architect
- Advantage Builder
- Advantage Laboratory
- Advantage Composer
- Advantage Scorekeeper
- Capability Genome Registry
- Advantage Retirement Office

## 5. Deployment Constraints
Modules move through strict lifecycles (Proposed -> Simulated -> Canaried -> Institutionalized). Unverified modules cannot execute external actions. Kill conditions automatically regress failing modules.
