## What the chronology shows

1. **The destination widened; authority did not.** From May to October the destination grew from a controlled refinery to a self-directed institution of Body, Organs and Mind. Every widening restated that shutdown, founder authority and the Consequence Gate still govern.
2. **Metaphor was re-read as effect.** Literal digital biology dominated the late-August and early-September sources. INTENT-0030 re-read it as functional capability formation, and the later records stay inside that reading.
3. **The bottleneck moved from features to convergence and proof.** By 2026-10-13 the lineage records divergence across PRs as the governing problem. The 2026-10-14 command asks for one trunk and one executed whole-machine trace, which this branch now has.
