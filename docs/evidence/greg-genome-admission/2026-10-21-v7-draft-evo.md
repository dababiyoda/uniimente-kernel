|---|---|

## Everything learned on dev seeds, disclosed

This is all that was seen before any freeze. None of it is admission evidence. Only dev seeds 0-9 of these
generators were ever generated (prototypes, `admission --dev`, tests).

**Development history (changes made after seeing dev results):**

1. GA prototype (scratch, not committed), random-neighbourhood NK only, uniform crossover: four GA settings
   (population 100/200, tournament 2/3/4) at 200 and 500 evaluations per bit against hill climbing went between
   1-8 and 6-4 on dev seeds 0-9. **The plain GA did not beat hill climbing on random NK.** I then added the trap and
   adjacent-NK kinds, knowing that deceptive traps with tight linkage favour crossover over bit-flip hill climbing,
   and chose two-point crossover for that reason. The suite is therefore partly chosen in the GA's favour against
   its baseline; the loose-linkage and random-NK kinds stay in it as the counter-case. No GA parameter was tuned
   after the module's first `--dev` run.
2. GA competitor choice on dev seeds 0-9 of a prototype NK-only generator (not the committed one), deterministic
   time 2.0: CP-SAT with element constraints proved 2/10 optimal and found no incumbent on 3/10; CP-SAT with table
   constraints found no incumbent on 2/10 but the better incumbent on 4 of the 7 seeds both solved (element better
   on 2, one tie); SciPy HiGHS on the marginal-polytope MILP (2 s wall) proved 3/10 optimal (the K=2 ones) with
   much weaker incumbents at K >= 3. **The module first shipped the element model** (a misreading of that
   comparison); on re-checking the numbers it was switched to the table model, the stronger competitor, after the
   first committed-generator `--dev` run (element: 4-5-1 against the GA; table: 3-6-1). No candidate or baseline
   setting changed with it.
3. MAP-Elites: after the first `--dev` run the answer exceeded the worker's 256 KiB output limit on dev seed 4
   (808 elites x 16 joints), so genomes are now rounded to 5 decimals with cells and qualities recomputed after
   rounding, and an abstention above grid^2 x joints = 16384 was added. The dev result was 10-0 against both arms
   before and after the change.
4. Centrality: the verifier's order check was relaxed from "strictly by value, ties by name" to "non-increasing
   within relative 1e-9" before the first dev run, because floating-point near-ties could otherwise reject a true
   answer.

**Dev-only expected verdicts (not part of the rule; reported negative-first):**

- `evo_ga_factored`: **EXPERIMENTAL is a likely outcome.** Its wins over hill climbing come from the trap kinds;
  on random NK hill climbing is as good or better. At 30 items, about 22-8 would be needed for p < 0.05; dev gave
  7-3. CP-SAT solves every trap instance exactly (traps are separable) and is mixed on NK, where it sometimes finds
  no incumbent (an abstention, quality 0), so against the competitor a loss short of significance is the dev
  picture (3-6-1); at 30 items a significant loss is possible, which would make a baseline-beating GA NICHE (it
  wins some items) rather than DEFAULT. If it beats the baseline and is not significantly worse than CP-SAT, the
  latency clause (0.03 s against 0.8 s) would make it DEFAULT; if it does not, it is EXPERIMENTAL and
  the coverage item stays at "built and benchmarked, no demonstrated edge". Neither NICHE nor DEFAULT would mean the
  GA beats an exact solver where the structure is solvable.
- `evo_map_elites`: DEFAULT_FOR_GEOMETRY expected, **but against a weak comparator.** A single-objective optimiser
  collapses onto the smoothest postures and covers few cells by design; beating it shows that QD covers the space,
  which is the point of the geometry, not that MAP-Elites is the best way to fill each cell. A per-cell targeted
  optimiser (one constrained solve per cell) was not built or measured and could beat it on per-cell quality.
- `graph_betweenness_topk`: DEFAULT_FOR_GEOMETRY expected **on the latency clause only**: it ties NetworkX on every
  dev item (both exact) and is about 6x faster in process. It does not answer anything NetworkX cannot. The degree
  baseline is `wrong` on every item partly because it claims no values; its set overlap (quality 0.375 mean, one
  exact set) is the meaningful comparison.

## What the outcome changes, and what it cannot

- A DEFAULT or NICHE verdict makes a family routable at its version by the Competency Compiler for its own inputs
  (`{num_bits, factors, evaluations, seed}`, `{link_lengths, grid, evaluations, seed}`, `{graph_edges, top_k}`). It
  does not attach it; attaching is the founder's decision.
- SUPERSEDED, EXPERIMENTAL and REJECTED exclude a family from routing; the code stays registered and withheld.
- All benchmarks are generated mathematical instances. Nothing here says anything about a real design, network or
  process yet.
