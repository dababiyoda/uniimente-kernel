These plumbing fixtures are not native benchmark execution. Existing authority, protected-learning, mutation and receipt-boundary tests remain separate release obligations, not results fabricated by this evaluator review. Learned routing is absent, so this evaluation cannot claim to resolve sparse-outcome feedback loops. Receipt verbosity and retention remain runtime costs outside this correction.

Reassessed advantages: failure diversity is wider within narrow linear/finite-sample/product scopes, not general intelligence; proof challengeability distinguishes a answer from its evidence without treating provisionality as deception; architectural reuse avoids a second router; learning remains conditional bookkeeping, with no learned-policy comparison; operation remains reversible and consequence-inert. Correlated verification/specification defects and central authority failure remain unresolved dependencies.
